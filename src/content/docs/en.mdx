## ## <a id="about-qiraati"></a>What Is Qiraati (QIRAATI)?

**Qiraati (QIRAATI)** is a Quran reading education method and a series of learning books developed to help students acquire the skills needed to read the Holy Quran correctly.

Originating in Indonesia, the Qiraati method has become widely known and used in Quran education, particularly in Southeast Asia.

The method is designed to guide learners from the very beginning of their reading journey towards accurate and fluent Quran recitation. It emphasizes understanding, observation, and practical reading exercises rather than relying primarily on memorization of numerous reading rules.

Students learn to recognize Arabic letters, vowel marks, and other reading symbols, then apply this knowledge through repeated reading practice.

An important goal of the method is to help students develop correct Quran recitation according to the rules of **Tajweed**, through guided practice and direct instruction.

## History of the Qiraati Method

The Qiraati method and its Quran reading books were developed by **KH. Dahlan Salim Zarkasyi**, an Islamic educator from Semarang, Indonesia.

The development of the method began in **1966**, with the aim of providing a structured and practical approach to teaching people how to read the Quran.

His teaching approach emphasized learning through understanding, observation, and repeated practice. Rather than making extensive memorization the starting point, students are encouraged to recognize reading patterns and apply them through practical exercises.

This approach contributed to the recognition and widespread use of the Qiraati method in Indonesia and other parts of Southeast Asia.

## Key Features of the Qiraati Method

The Qiraati method focuses on developing Quran reading skills through structured lessons and practical exercises.

### 1. Suitable for Complete Beginners

Students can start learning from the basics, even if they have little or no previous experience with Arabic letters or Quran reading.

### 2. Learning Through Understanding

Students learn to recognize Arabic letters, vowel marks, and reading symbols. They develop an understanding of how these elements affect pronunciation and reading.

### 3. Practical Reading Exercises

The method emphasizes actual reading practice. Students repeatedly read examples and exercises to improve their accuracy, confidence, and fluency.

### 4. Learning Tajweed Through Practice

Students are guided to develop correct Quran recitation according to Tajweed principles. The emphasis is on applying correct reading techniques through practice rather than beginning with extensive memorization of theoretical rules.

### 5. Step-by-Step Learning

The five-book learning series follows a progressive structure, starting with basic reading skills and gradually introducing more advanced material.

This allows students to build their knowledge and reading ability systematically.

## Who Can Learn the Qiraati Method?

The Qiraati method is suitable for people of all ages and backgrounds who wish to learn how to read the Holy Quran.

It can be particularly helpful for:

- Children who are beginning their Quran education.
- Teenagers who want to develop their Quran reading skills.
- Adults who have never learned to read the Quran.
- New Muslims who wish to begin learning Quran reading.
- Existing learners who want to improve their pronunciation and recitation.

No advanced Arabic knowledge is required to begin. Students can start with the basic lessons and gradually develop their reading abilities.

Regular practice and guidance from a knowledgeable Quran teacher are recommended to achieve the best learning results.

## How to Use Qiraati Web

Qiraati Web is designed as a supplementary learning resource for students studying the Qiraati method.

You can use the website to review lessons, listen to reading examples where available, and practice the material covered in your classes.

We recommend the following learning approach:

### Step 1: Learn with a Qualified Teacher

Begin by studying the lessons with a knowledgeable Quran teacher who can explain the material and demonstrate the correct pronunciation.

### Step 2: Review Lessons Online

Visit Qiraati Web and open the corresponding book and lesson to review what you have learned in class.

### Step 3: Practice Regularly

Read the examples and exercises repeatedly. Consistent practice helps develop familiarity, accuracy, and reading fluency.

### Step 4: Ask Your Teacher to Check Your Reading

Read the lessons aloud to your teacher so that any pronunciation mistakes or reading errors can be identified and corrected.

### An Important Note About Self-Study

Qiraati Web is intended to support Quran learning and independent practice. However, learning through a website alone cannot fully replace guidance from a qualified Quran teacher.

Correct Quran recitation requires proper pronunciation of Arabic letters, accurate application of Tajweed rules, and listening-based correction.

Students are therefore encouraged to combine online practice with direct instruction from a knowledgeable teacher.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Qiraati

### What is the Qiraati method?

Qiraati is a Quran reading education method developed in Indonesia. It uses structured lessons and practical exercises to help students learn to read the Holy Quran accurately and fluently.

### Can complete beginners learn to read the Quran with Qiraati?

Yes. The Qiraati method is designed to help beginners start from the basics. Students gradually learn Arabic letters, vowel marks, reading patterns, and more advanced Quran reading skills.

### How many books are in the Qiraati course?

The Qiraati learning series featured on this website consists of five books. They are arranged from basic to more advanced reading levels.

### Is Qiraati suitable for adults?

Yes. Qiraati can be used by children, teenagers, and adults. It is suitable for anyone who wants to begin learning Quran reading or improve their existing skills.

### Can I learn the Qiraati method online?

You can use Qiraati Web to review lessons and practice Quran reading online. However, we strongly recommend learning with a qualified teacher who can listen to your recitation and correct any mistakes.

### Does the Qiraati method teach Tajweed?

Yes. The method aims to develop correct Quran reading according to Tajweed principles through practical reading exercises and teacher guidance, rather than relying primarily on memorizing theoretical rules at the beginning.

### Do I need to know Arabic before starting Qiraati?

No. You do not need advanced Arabic knowledge to begin. The course starts with basic reading skills and progresses gradually.

### How long does it take to complete the Qiraati course?

The time required varies depending on each student's learning pace, practice frequency, and teacher guidance. Regular practice and consistent lessons are important for making progress.
